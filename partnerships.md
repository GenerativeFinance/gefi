GeFi is built as a marketplace, which means we only work if other people
build on top of us, route customers to us, or feed regulated data into us.
If you operate in one of the three areas below, we want to talk.

## Technology partners

Cloud and edge infrastructure, model hosting, vector databases, observability,
and security tooling. If your platform touches model inference, training, or
audit, we likely have a customer who needs both.

- Joint reference architectures and co-authored deployment guides.
- Shared go-to-market for sovereign and regulated workloads.
- Marketplace listing with a self-serve integration page in our docs.

## Distribution partners

Consultancies, system integrators, regional resellers, and managed-service
providers who already sit inside banks, exchanges, asset managers, and
fintechs. We pay rev-share on subscriptions you originate and on the
federated revenue earned by the models you deploy.

- Tiered partner programme with deal registration.
- Per-jurisdiction enablement (EU AI Act, MAS FEAT, ADGM, FCA).
- Co-marketing budget for joint customer events.

## Institutional data partners

Exchanges, custodians, market-data vendors, KYC/AML providers, and research
houses with proprietary, regulated datasets. Contribute via federated
training and earn a share of every inference call your data improves —
without your raw data ever leaving your perimeter.

- Differential-privacy budgets per dataset, surfaced in the dashboard.
- Hash-chained provenance for every gradient contribution.
- Revocation in one click, with automatic re-train.
