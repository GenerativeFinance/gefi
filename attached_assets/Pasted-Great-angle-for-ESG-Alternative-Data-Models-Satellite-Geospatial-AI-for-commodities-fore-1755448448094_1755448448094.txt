## 🔹 **UI Layout for ESG & Geospatial AI (Commodities Forecasting)**

### **1. Input Panel (Left Sidebar)**

* **Data Sources**

  * Upload geospatial datasets (satellite imagery, shapefiles, weather data)
  * API connectors: NASA EarthData, ESA Copernicus, Google Earth Engine
* **Model Options**

  * Forecasting target: Oil, Wheat, Corn, Coffee, etc.
  * AI model: CNN (image-based), Hybrid (CNN + LSTM for time series), Transformer-based
* **Analysis Controls**

  * Geographic focus: select region/country on map
  * Time horizon: 1 month / 3 months / 1 year forecast
  * Factors to include: Vegetation, Deforestation, Rainfall, Temperature anomalies

---

### **2. Main Dashboard (Center Panel)**

* **Interactive Geospatial Map** 🌍

  * Satellite imagery overlay (NDVI for crop health, oil rig activity, mining sites)
  * Heatmaps of production zones & climate stress areas
  * Time-lapse slider for historical imagery (trend visualization)
* **Commodity Forecast Chart** 📈

  * Line chart: historical vs AI-forecasted commodity prices
  * Confidence bands for uncertainty
* **Supply Chain Risk Map**

  * Icons showing key production/export hubs
  * Disruption signals (e.g., drought, wildfires, port congestion)

---

### **3. Insights Panel (Right Sidebar)**

* **Forecast Drivers (Explainability)**

  * Top features influencing prediction (rainfall ↓, deforestation ↑, soil health ↓)
  * SHAP-style bar chart for feature importance
* **Risk Assessment**

  * Probability of supply disruption
  * ESG red flags (illegal mining, deforestation zones, water scarcity hotspots)
* **Scenario Testing**

  * Adjust climate scenarios (El Niño, global warming +2°C, trade sanctions)
  * Forecast updates in real time

---

### **4. Export & Reporting**

* Export commodity forecast data (CSV/Excel/JSON)
* Download annotated geospatial maps (PNG/PDF)
* Generate **AI-written ESG & Commodities Report**: key risks, hotspots, price implications
* API integration into **trading & risk systems**
