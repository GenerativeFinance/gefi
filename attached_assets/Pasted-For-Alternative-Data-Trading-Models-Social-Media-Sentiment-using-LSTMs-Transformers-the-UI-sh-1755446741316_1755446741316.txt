For **Alternative Data Trading Models (Social Media Sentiment using LSTMs/Transformers)**, the UI should look like a **sentiment trading intelligence dashboard**. It should merge **real-time feeds (Twitter, Reddit, news)** with **AI-driven sentiment analysis** and **market impact visualization**.

## 🔹 **UI Layout for Social Media Sentiment Trading Models (LSTMs/Transformers)**

### **1. Input Panel (Left Sidebar)**

* **Data Sources**

  * Toggle to include: Twitter, Reddit (r/wallstreetbets, crypto subs), News APIs
  * Upload custom text datasets (CSV/JSON with timestamps + text)
* **Model Settings**

  * Choose NLP model: LSTM | Transformer | FinBERT | Hybrid (sentiment + embeddings)
  * Sentiment granularity: Document-level | Sentence-level | Word-level
  * Filter by ticker, sector, or hashtags (e.g., \$TSLA, \$BTC, #AIStocks)
* **Analysis Window**

  * Time horizon: intraday, 1-day, 7-day rolling sentiment
  * Option for real-time stream vs historical backtest

---

### **2. Main Dashboard (Center Panel)**

* **Sentiment Timeline Chart**

  * Line chart: sentiment score vs time
  * Overlay stock/crypto price for correlation
* **Word Cloud / Keyword Heatmap**

  * Most influential words/hashtags driving sentiment
  * Color-coded (green = positive, red = negative)
* **Ticker/Asset Sentiment Comparison**

  * Bar chart: sentiment across multiple assets (TSLA, AAPL, BTC, ETH)
  * Bubble chart: sentiment vs trading volume (size = chatter intensity)
* **Market Impact Forecast**

  * Probability gauge: “High chance of volatility in next 24h”
  * Monte Carlo fan chart for price impact

---

### **3. Insights Panel (Right Sidebar)**

* **Top Mentions & Sources**

  * Table: Ticker | Sentiment Score | Mention Volume | Source (Twitter/Reddit/News)
* **Explainability Layer**

  * Highlight phrases driving model predictions (attention heatmap for Transformers)
  * Feature importance (e.g., “short squeeze” ↑, “earnings beat” ↑, “bankruptcy” ↓)
* **Trading Signal Generator**

  * Buy / Hold / Sell signals with confidence levels
  * AI narrative summary: *“Positive sentiment surge for TSLA driven by earnings call chatter.”*

---

### **4. Export & Reporting**

* Export sentiment scores per ticker (CSV/Excel/JSON)
* Download visuals (timeline charts, word clouds) as PNG/PDF
* Generate **AI-written Social Sentiment Report**: bullish/bearish signals, chatter hotspots, risk events
* API/webhook to send signals into trading systems
