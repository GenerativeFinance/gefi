For **Portfolio Optimization & Asset Allocation (Hierarchical Risk Parity with AI adjustments)** the UI should look like a **portfolio manager’s cockpit** — clean enough for executives to understand, but detailed enough for quants to explore risk decomposition, allocation structure, and performance scenarios.

---

## 🔹 **UI Layout for Portfolio Optimization & HRP (AI-enhanced)**

### **1. Input Panel (Left Sidebar)**

* **Data Upload / Feeds**

  * Upload portfolio (tickers, weights, volatilities, returns) via CSV/Excel
  * API connectors (Bloomberg, Yahoo Finance, Quandl)
* **Optimization Settings**

  * Method selector: Mean-Variance | Risk Parity | HRP | HRP + AI adjustment
  * Constraints: max/min weights, sector caps, ESG exclusions
  * Risk tolerance slider (Conservative ↔ Aggressive)
* **AI Adjustment Controls**

  * Toggle AI factor adjustments (macro risk, sentiment, ESG tilt)
  * Scenario selector: *Base Case*, *Inflation Shock*, *Recession*, *Bull Market*

---

### **2. Main Dashboard (Center Panel)**

* **Portfolio Allocation Visualization**

  * Treemap or Sunburst (weights by asset/sector)
  * Pie chart comparison: HRP allocation vs Original portfolio
* **Correlation & Clustering**

  * Correlation heatmap of assets
  * HRP dendrogram (tree structure of clustering)
* **Performance Metrics**

  * Cards showing: Expected Return, Volatility, Sharpe Ratio, VaR, CVaR
* **Scenario Simulation**

  * Line chart of portfolio returns under different scenarios
  * Monte Carlo fan chart for distribution of returns

---

### **3. Insights Panel (Right Sidebar)**

* **Risk Contribution Breakdown**

  * Table: Asset | Weight % | Risk Contribution % | AI Adjustment
* **Factor Exposures**

  * Bar chart of factor risks (Market, Value, Momentum, ESG tilt, Macro shocks)
* **Explainability Layer**

  * AI narrative explaining over/underweights
  * “Top Drivers of Allocation Changes”

---

### **4. Export & Reporting (Footer or Top Bar)**

* Export portfolio allocation (CSV, Excel, JSON)
* Export visuals (PNG, PDF)
* Generate **AI-written portfolio report**: allocation rationale, risks, scenario insights

---

✅ This UI allows:

* **Quants** → Deep dive into risk decomposition, clustering, and optimization.
* **Executives/Clients** → Clean visuals of allocations, risks, and what-if scenarios.
