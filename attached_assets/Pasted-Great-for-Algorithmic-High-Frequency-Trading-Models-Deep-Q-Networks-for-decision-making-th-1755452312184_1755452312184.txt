## 🔹 **UI Layout for Algo/HFT with DQN Decision-Making**

### **1. Input Panel (Left Sidebar)**

* **Market Data Feeds**

  * Connect to real-time market data (stocks, FX, crypto, futures)
  * Upload historical data for backtesting
* **Model Options**

  * Choose strategy type (market-making, momentum, mean reversion, pairs trading)
  * Model toggle: Baseline (rule-based) vs DQN vs Hybrid
* **Trading Parameters**

  * Position size limits (min/max order size)
  * Leverage & risk controls
  * Stop-loss / take-profit thresholds
* **Execution Mode**

  * Backtest / Paper Trading / Live Execution

---

### **2. Main Dashboard (Center Panel)**

* **Market View**

  * Live candlestick chart with bid-ask spread overlay
  * Order book depth chart (Level 2 market data)
* **DQN Strategy Decisions**

  * Highlight buy/sell/hold actions on chart
  * Confidence levels (action-value Q-scores)
* **PnL & Performance Tracking**

  * Real-time equity curve (profits & losses)
  * Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, win rate
* **Latency & Execution Monitoring**

  * Trade execution time histogram
  * Slippage analysis

---

### **3. Insights Panel (Right Sidebar)**

* **Trade Log (Chronological)**

  * Time | Action | Instrument | Size | Price | PnL | Q-Value
* **State & Feature Analysis**

  * Heatmap of DQN state representations (features influencing decision)
  * Feature importance ranking (e.g., order flow imbalance, volatility)
* **Scenario Stress Testing**

  * Replay of extreme events (flash crash, rate hike, sudden volatility spike)
  * Model decisions vs benchmark strategies

---

### **4. Export & Reporting**

* Export trade logs (CSV/Excel/JSON)
* Download performance charts (PNG/PDF)
* Generate **AI-written Trading Report**: strategy summary, risk metrics, anomaly trades
* API integration with broker/exchange for execution
