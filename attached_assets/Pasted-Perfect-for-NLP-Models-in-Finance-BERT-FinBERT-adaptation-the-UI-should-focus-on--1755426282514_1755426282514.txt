## 🔹 **UI Layout for NLP Models in Finance (BERT / FinBERT)**

### **1. Input Panel (Left Sidebar)**

* **Data Input Options**

  * Upload financial documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT)
  * Paste financial news/article text
  * API connector for real-time news & filings (Bloomberg, Reuters, SEC EDGAR)
* **Model Selection**

  * BERT (generic)
  * FinBERT (finance-specific)
  * Sentiment only / Named Entity Recognition (NER) / Topic Classification
* **Analysis Scope**

  * Whole document vs paragraph vs sentence-level analysis

---

### **2. Main Dashboard (Center Panel)**

* **Text Sentiment Visualization**

  * Highlighted text with color-coded sentiment (green = positive, red = negative, gray = neutral)
  * Sentence-level sentiment bar chart (positive/negative/neutral %)
* **Entity & Keyword Extraction**

  * Table/Tag cloud of key entities (companies, people, tickers, sectors)
  * Frequency ranking of financial terms (e.g., "inflation," "earnings," "merger")
* **Trend Tracking**

  * Time-series line chart of sentiment across multiple documents (e.g., company mentions sentiment over time)
* **Comparative Mode**

  * Compare sentiment across companies/sectors (e.g., Tesla vs Ford in news coverage)

---

### **3. Insights Panel (Right Sidebar)**

* **Risk & Opportunity Flags**

  * AI-detected red flags: bankruptcy mentions, lawsuits, credit downgrades
  * Opportunity signals: strong earnings, upgrades, M\&A activity
* **Explainability (XAI for NLP)**

  * Word importance visualization (which words influenced sentiment classification most)
  * Attention heatmap (which parts of the document mattered most)
* **Custom Taxonomies**

  * Map terms to financial categories (Macro, Credit, Equity, ESG, Commodities)

---

### **4. Export & Reporting**

* Export results: CSV/Excel (entities, sentiment scores, topics)
* Download annotated document (PDF with highlights)
* Generate **AI-written executive summary** of sentiment & key insights

---

✅ This UI lets analysts **quickly process large amounts of financial text** and extract **actionable insights for trading, credit, or risk management**.
