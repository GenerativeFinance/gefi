## 🔹 **UI Layout for Liquidity Mining & Yield Farming Optimizers**

### **1. Input Panel (Left Sidebar)**

* **Wallet & Protocol Connections**

  * Connect wallet (MetaMask, WalletConnect, Ledger)
  * Select protocols to include (Uniswap, Curve, Aave, Compound, Balancer, Yearn)
* **Investment Preferences**

  * Capital allocation amount (slider or input)
  * Risk tolerance (Low / Medium / High)
  * Preferred chain(s): Ethereum, BSC, Polygon, Solana, etc.
* **AI Optimization Options**

  * Strategy type: Yield Maximization | Risk-adjusted | Stablecoin-only | Multi-chain
  * Reinforcement Learning toggle (dynamic rebalancing vs static allocation)

---

### **2. Main Dashboard (Center Panel)**

* **Portfolio Yield Overview**

  * APY visualization (current vs optimized AI allocation)
  * Stacked bar chart of returns by protocol & token
* **Protocol Comparison Table**

  * Protocol | Pool | Token Pair | APY | Impermanent Loss Risk | AI Score
* **Risk-Adjusted Allocation Visualization**

  * Pie chart: Recommended capital split across pools
  * Sunburst chart: Pool → Protocol → Chain distribution
* **Forecast Simulation**

  * Monte Carlo projection of expected returns
  * Stress test under adverse conditions (e.g., token price crash, liquidity shock)

---

### **3. Insights Panel (Right Sidebar)**

* **Risk & Reward Breakdown**

  * Table: Pool | Expected APY | Risk Score | Volatility Impact
* **Explainability Layer**

  * AI narrative explaining why certain pools are chosen/avoided
  * “Top Drivers” (TVL growth, historical APY stability, on-chain governance risks)
* **Protocol Health Indicators**

  * Smart contract audit status (✅ audited / ⚠ risky)
  * Historical exploit tracker
  * Governance centralization risk

---

### **4. Export & Reporting**

* Export optimized allocation as CSV/JSON
* Download risk-return charts as PNG/PDF
* Generate **AI-written DeFi Yield Report**: recommended pools, risks, projected returns
* Option to **auto-deploy strategy via wallet integration**
