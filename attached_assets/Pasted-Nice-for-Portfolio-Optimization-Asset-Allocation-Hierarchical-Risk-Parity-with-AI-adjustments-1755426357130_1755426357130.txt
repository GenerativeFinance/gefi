---

## 🔹 **UI Layout for Portfolio Optimization & HRP (AI-enhanced)**

### **1. Input Panel (Left Sidebar)**

* **Data Sources**

  * Upload portfolio (tickers, weights, returns, volatilities) via CSV/Excel
  * API connectors: Bloomberg, Yahoo Finance, Quandl
* **Optimization Options**

  * Choose method: Mean-Variance, Risk Parity, HRP, HRP + AI adjustment
  * Constraints (max weight, min weight, sector/asset restrictions)
  * Risk tolerance slider (conservative ↔ aggressive)
* **AI Adjustment Controls**

  * Toggle for AI factor adjustments (macro risk, sentiment, ESG tilt)
  * Scenario selection: "Base Case, Inflation Shock, Recession, Bull Market"

---

### **2. Main Dashboard (Center Panel)**

* **Portfolio Allocation Visualization**

  * Sunburst chart / Treemap of portfolio weights by asset/sector
  * Pie chart for HRP allocation vs. original portfolio
* **Correlation & Clustering**

  * Dendrogram (HRP tree structure)
  * Correlation heatmap of assets
* **Performance Metrics**

  * Expected Return, Volatility, Sharpe Ratio
  * Value at Risk (VaR), Conditional VaR
* **Scenario Simulation**

  * Line chart: portfolio returns under different market regimes
  * Monte Carlo simulation results (fan chart)

---

### **3. Insights Panel (Right Sidebar)**

* **Risk Contribution Breakdown**

  * Table: Asset | Allocation % | Risk Contribution % | AI Adjustment
* **Factor Exposure View**

  * Bar chart: Exposure to factors (Market, Size, Momentum, ESG tilt, Macro risks)
* **Explainability Layer**

  * AI-generated explanation of why certain assets were overweight/underweight
  * “Top Drivers of Allocation Changes”

---

### **4. Export & Reporting**

* Export optimized portfolio as CSV/Excel/JSON
* Download allocation charts as PNG/PDF
* Generate **AI-written Portfolio Report**: allocation rationale, risk drivers, stress test results

---

✅ This UI helps **portfolio managers understand allocation decisions**, while also giving **executives & clients a clean view of diversification and risk control**.
