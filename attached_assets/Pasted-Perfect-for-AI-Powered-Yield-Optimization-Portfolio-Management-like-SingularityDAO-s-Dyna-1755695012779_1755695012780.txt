Perfect — for **AI-Powered Yield Optimization & Portfolio Management** (like **SingularityDAO’s DynaSets**), the UI needs to look and feel like a **quant-driven robo-advisor**, where users can **delegate capital to AI-managed token baskets** while still understanding *why* the AI makes allocation changes.

Here’s the optimal design 👇

---

## 🔹 **UI Layout: AI Yield Optimization & Portfolio Management (DynaSets)**

### **1. Input Panel (Left Sidebar)**

* **Portfolio Onboarding**

  * Deposit assets (ETH, stablecoins, tokens)
  * Select target portfolio type (Growth | Balanced | Defensive | Custom)
* **Strategy Options**

  * Choose DynaSet: BTC/ETH growth, DeFi yield, Stable yield, Altcoin basket
  * AI toggle: “AI dynamic rebalancing ON/OFF”
  * Constraints: Max drawdown %, max token weight %, exclude tokens/sectors
* **Risk & Preferences**

  * Risk slider (Conservative ↔ Aggressive)
  * ESG/DeFi filter (exclude certain assets or protocols)

---

### **2. Main Dashboard (Center Panel)**

* **Portfolio Allocation Visualization**

  * Pie chart of current allocations
  * Sunburst/treemap of allocations by sector (L1s, DeFi, stablecoins, etc.)
  * Comparison: User-selected weights vs AI-optimized weights
* **Performance Metrics**

  * ROI (since inception, YTD, last 30d)
  * Volatility, Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio
  * Yield decomposition (trading gains vs yield farming vs staking rewards)
* **Rebalancing Activity Feed**

  * Timeline: “AI sold 5% ETH → added 5% to stables due to volatility spike”
  * Tags for rationale: *Momentum, Correlation, Liquidity*

---

### **3. Insights & AI Explainability (Right Sidebar)**

* **AI Allocation Rationale**

  * Text explanation: *“AI increased exposure to BTC due to decreasing correlation with ETH and improving momentum signals.”*
* **Risk Contribution Breakdown**

  * Table: Asset | Allocation % | Risk Contribution % | Yield Contribution %
* **Scenario Simulations**

  * Stress tests: *Inflation shock, Recession, Bull market, Stablecoin depeg*
  * Line chart projections of portfolio value under scenarios

---

### **4. DynaSet Marketplace & Social Layer**

* **Marketplace of AI Portfolios**

  * Browse different DynaSets (AI-managed baskets) ranked by performance, risk, strategy
  * Copy-invest option (“Mirror this DynaSet”)
* **Community Ratings & Leaderboards**

  * Top-performing portfolios ranked by ROI, Sharpe, risk-adjusted return
  * User reviews on strategy transparency & stability

---

### **5. Export & Reporting**

* Export portfolio history (CSV/Excel/JSON)
* Download AI strategy report (PDF) → allocation changes, rationale, performance vs benchmark
* Auto-generate client-friendly summary report with charts

---

✅ This UI balances **quantitative transparency** (allocations, risk, performance) with **executive clarity** (clean visuals, scenario testing, simple explanations).
It makes it feel like you’re investing in a **living, AI-managed ETF** inside DeFi.
