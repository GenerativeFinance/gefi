For a user profile on GeFi, a marketplace for AI financial models, the information included should provide a comprehensive overview of the user's identity, expertise, credibility, and activity on the platform. This is essential for building trust and enabling meaningful interactions among users, whether they are model creators, investors, or other stakeholders. Below is a detailed breakdown of the key information to include, tailored to the needs of GeFi's ecosystem:

---

### **Basic Profile Details**
These foundational elements establish the user's identity and presence on the platform:
- **Full Name**: Display the user's full name (e.g., "Guillaume Lauzier") to personalize the profile and establish identity.
- **Profile Picture**: Include a professional photo or initials to make the profile visually appealing and recognizable.
- **Bio**: A short description (e.g., 50-100 words) summarizing the user's expertise, experience, and goals on GeFi (e.g., "AI developer specializing in predictive stock models with 5+ years in finance").
- **Joined Date**: Show when the user joined the platform (e.g., "Joined: 13/07/2025") to indicate their tenure.
- **Last Active**: Indicate the most recent activity (e.g., "Last Active: 2 days ago") to reflect engagement.

---

### **Role-Specific Information**
GeFi supports multiple user roles, such as Investors, Developers, Data Providers, and Regulators. Clearly indicating the user's role(s) and providing role-specific details helps others understand their focus and contributions:
- **Role(s)**: List the user's primary role(s) on the platform (e.g., "Developer," "Investor," "Data Provider"). Users may have multiple roles, so allow for flexibility.
- **For Developers**:
  - **Models Created**: A list of AI financial models they’ve developed, including brief descriptions and key metrics (e.g., "Stock Predictor: 85% accuracy, 12% ROI").
  - **Model Ratings**: Average user ratings or reviews for their models (e.g., "4.8/5 based on 50 reviews").
  - **Certifications**: Relevant credentials (e.g., "Certified in Machine Learning by Coursera").
- **For Investors**:
  - **Portfolio Performance**: Key metrics like return on investment (ROI), annualized returns, or risk-adjusted returns (e.g., "Portfolio ROI: 15% over 6 months").
  - **Trading Activity**: Number of trades executed and success rate (e.g., "200 trades, 78% success rate").
  - **Investment Focus**: Preferred asset classes or sectors (e.g., "Specializes in crypto and equities").
- **For Data Providers**:
  - **Data Sets Provided**: Number and type of data sets contributed (e.g., "5 datasets: stock prices, sentiment data").
  - **Data Quality Metrics**: Indicators like accuracy or timeliness (e.g., "98% data completeness").
  - **Compliance**: Adherence to standards (e.g., "GDPR Compliant").
- **For Regulators**:
  - **Compliance Oversight**: Areas of focus (e.g., "AI model fairness, data privacy").
  - **Certifications**: Relevant qualifications (e.g., "Certified Compliance Officer").

---

### **Performance and Activity Metrics**
Quantifiable metrics showcase the user's expertise and reliability, fostering trust within the marketplace:
- **General User Rating**: An overall rating based on interactions or contributions (e.g., "4.9/5 from 100 reviews").
- **Engagement Metrics**: Community participation rate (e.g., "87% active in forums") or similar indicators of platform involvement.
- **Trading Scoring (for Investors)**:
  - **Success Rate**: Percentage of profitable trades (e.g., "75% trade success").
  - **Profitability Score**: A composite score reflecting gains (e.g., "Profitability: 82/100").
  - **Risk Profile**: A classification like "Low Risk" or "High Risk" based on trading behavior.
- **Model Performance (for Developers)**:
  - **Average Accuracy**: Across all models (e.g., "Model Accuracy: 88%").
  - **Average ROI**: Financial impact of models (e.g., "Average ROI: 10%").
  - **Success Rate**: Predictive reliability (e.g., "Success Rate: 90%").
- **Activity Badges**: Achievements that highlight milestones (e.g., "Top-Rated Developer," "Consistent Trader").

---

### **Security and Compliance**
Given the financial and data-sensitive nature of GeFi, these elements enhance trustworthiness:
- **Compliance Badges**: Display certifications like "GDPR Compliant," "ISO 27001 Certified," or "SOC 2 Type II Compliant" if applicable to the user’s role.
- **Verification Status**: Indicate platform verification (e.g., "Verified Developer") to confirm authenticity.

---

### **Interaction and Collaboration**
Facilitate connections between users to support the marketplace ecosystem:
- **Contact Options**: Enable messaging or connection requests (e.g., "Message User" button) for collaboration opportunities.
- **Portfolio or Model Links**: Provide direct links to the user’s models or investment portfolio for easy access (e.g., "View Models," "Explore Portfolio").

---

### **User Preferences**
Allow customization to enhance the user experience:
- **Dashboard Mode**: Let users select their preferred view (e.g., "Investor Mode" or "Developer Mode") to tailor the interface.
- **Notification Settings**: Options for alerts on model updates, trade executions, or messages (e.g., "Notify me of new ratings").

### **Final Advice**
To make the profile effective for GeFi:
- **Prioritize Clarity**: Use concise, scannable sections with visual aids (e.g., icons, charts) for metrics.
- **Highlight Strengths**: Emphasize performance and ratings to attract collaboration or investment.

This structure ensures that GeFi user profiles are informative, trustworthy, and aligned with the platform’s focus on AI financial models, catering to all key roles and use cases.
