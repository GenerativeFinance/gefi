Here’s the breakdown of what the UI should include:

---

## 🔹 **UI Layout for Risk Management & Credit Models (DNN for PD/LGD)**

### **1. Input Panel (Left Sidebar)**

* **Data Upload / API Connectors**

  * Upload loan/credit datasets (CSV/Excel)
  * API connectors to banking systems / credit bureaus
* **Model Options**

  * Choose between: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, DNN
  * Select Target Variable: PD (Probability of Default), LGD (Loss Given Default), or EAD (Exposure at Default)
  * Train/Test Split selector
  * Hyperparameter settings (layers, neurons, activation functions, learning rate)
* **Feature Selection**

  * Checkbox list of borrower features: income, DTI ratio, credit score, employment length, collateral value

---

### **2. Main Dashboard (Center Panel)**

* **Risk Heatmap / Distribution**

  * Histogram of predicted PD across borrowers
  * Distribution of LGD estimates
* **Credit Scoring Chart (Scatter / Bubble)**

  * X-axis: Credit Score
  * Y-axis: Predicted PD
  * Bubble size = Loan Amount
  * Color = Risk Tier (Low/Med/High)
* **Model Accuracy & Metrics**

  * ROC Curve & AUC score
  * Confusion Matrix (for PD classification)
  * Regression metrics for LGD (R², RMSE, MAE)

---

### **3. Insights Panel (Right Sidebar)**

* **Borrower-Level Risk Breakdown**

  * Table: Borrower ID | PD (%) | LGD (%) | Risk Tier | Recommendation
* **Explainability (XAI Layer)**

  * SHAP/Feature importance chart → explains what drove the model’s decision
  * “Top Risk Drivers” per borrower or portfolio segment
* **Portfolio Risk View**

  * Pie chart of loans by risk tier
  * Stress-testing sliders (e.g., unemployment rate ↑, interest rate ↑) → dynamic update of PD/LGD

---

### **4. Export & Reporting**

* Export borrower-level PD/LGD results to Excel/CSV/JSON
* Generate automated credit risk report (summary of portfolio health & high-risk segments)
* API endpoint to send PD/LGD outputs into risk systems

---

✅ This UI would let **credit analysts drill down on individual borrowers** while also letting **executives see portfolio-wide risk trends**.
