3. Detailed UI Design by Model

Stock Prediction Model

Header:
Title: "Stock Prediction Model"
Description: "AI-driven stock price forecasting"
Metrics Row:
"1,200 Subscribers" (person icon, blue)
"85% Accuracy" (target icon, green)
"90th Percentile Speed" (gauge icon, purple)
"12% Returns" (dollar sign icon, green)
Graphs and Charts:
Line Graph: Returns over the last year (X-axis: months, Y-axis: percentage returns). A green line trends upward, showing 12% annualized return, with markers for model updates.
Bar Chart: Accuracy comparison with competing models (X-axis: model names, Y-axis: accuracy). A blue bar at 85% alongside others (e.g., 80%, 90%) in green and purple.
Scatter Plot: Predicted vs. actual returns (X-axis: predicted, Y-axis: actual). Green points cluster near a diagonal line, showing 85% accuracy, with outliers in red.
Interactive Features:
Tooltips: Hover over "85% Accuracy" to see "Based on 12-month backtesting."
Filters: Dropdowns for time periods (e.g., "Last 6 Months") and regions.
