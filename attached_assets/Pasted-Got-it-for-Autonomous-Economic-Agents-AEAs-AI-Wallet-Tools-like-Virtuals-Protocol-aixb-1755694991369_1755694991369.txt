## 🔹 **UI Layout: Autonomous Economic Agents & AI Wallet Tools**

### **1. Agent Control Panel (Left Sidebar)**

* **Agent Management**

  * Create New Agent (choose strategy: trading, yield farming, arbitrage, governance, liquidity provision)
  * Import/Export agent (via wallet/private key or mnemonic)
  * Agent templates library (pre-built strategies, e.g., “Conservative Yield Farmer,” “Aggressive Arbitrageur”)
* **Wallet Integration**

  * Connect DeFi wallet (MetaMask, Phantom, WalletConnect)
  * Multi-chain toggle (Ethereum, Solana, Polygon, BSC)
* **AI Settings**

  * Select AI model: RL agent, Bayesian optimizer, Neural net, GPT-based decision support
  * Risk tolerance slider (low ↔ high)
  * Automation toggle: Manual Confirmation | Semi-Autonomous | Fully Autonomous

---

### **2. Main Dashboard (Center Panel)**

* **Agent Activity Feed**

  * Timeline of trades, staking, yield harvests, governance votes executed by agents
  * Real-time P\&L tracking per agent
* **Portfolio & Position View**

  * Allocations across chains, tokens, pools, lending platforms
  * Pie chart: asset allocation per agent
  * Stacked bar: yield sources (trading vs farming vs staking vs lending)
* **Decision Justification**

  * AI explainability panel: “Agent allocated 10% to Curve pool due to rising APY & stablecoin inflow.”

---

### **3. Insights & Risk Panel (Right Sidebar)**

* **Performance Analytics**

  * ROI, Sharpe ratio, volatility per agent
  * Benchmark comparison vs BTC/ETH or passive LP strategies
* **Risk Alerts**

  * Notifications: *“High gas fee detected, delaying transaction.”*
  * Protocol security flags: rug pull risk, liquidity drain detection
* **Governance Voting Preview**

  * If an agent participates in DAO votes → show proposed decision & rationale

---

### **4. Agent Marketplace & Collaboration**

* **Strategy Marketplace**

  * Browse & deploy shared AI agents created by other users (rated & ranked by performance)
  * “Clone Agent” option to copy someone else’s bot and tweak settings
* **Social Layer**

  * Leaderboard of top-performing agents
  * Community rating of strategies

---

### **5. Export & Reporting**

* Export transaction logs (CSV/JSON)
* Download AI agent strategy report (PDF) → portfolio breakdown, logic, risk drivers
* API/Webhook: integrate alerts into Telegram, Discord, Slack

---

✅ This UI would make it possible for a **non-technical DeFi user to launch and monitor their own AI DeFi agent**, while still giving **quant-level transparency** to advanced users.
