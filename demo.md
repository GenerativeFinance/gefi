A demo is the fastest way to see how GeFi handles **your** model, **your**

## What you'll see

- A live run of one of our **federated models** against a sample of your
  data (or a representative public dataset, if you'd rather not share).
- The **audit log** in action: every inference call hash-chained, with a

- The **compliance routing** flow for the jurisdictions you operate in —
  US, EU, UK, MENA, APAC — and how requests are routed to the right counsel
  and auditor.
- A walkthrough of the **pricing** tiers ([Free, Starter, Pro,
  Institutional, Enterprise](/pricing/)) and which one is the right fit
  given your inference volume and KYB posture.

who need explainable, audit-ready AI in a regulated workflow. If you're
just experimenting, the [Free tier](/pricing/) is probably a faster way
to start; come back when you're ready to put a model into production.
