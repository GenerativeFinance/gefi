How it works

Four steps from "interesting model" to "in production".

  • 01

    Discover

    Browse 60+ models across investing, risk, fraud, and trade finance. Filter by jurisdiction, federated status, or risk class.

  • 02

    Subscribe

    Pick a tier, complete KYC if needed, and get an API key in under five minutes. Pay by card or onchain.

  • 03

    Run

    Call the model from your stack, the GeFi dashboard, or our paper-trading sandbox. Every call is logged and provable.

  • 04

    Audit

    Pull a Merkle proof for any run, export a compliance pack for your jurisdiction, and ship it to your regulator.
